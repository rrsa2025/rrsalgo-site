What we do differently

We don't force plays

If nothing clears our bar, we say so. Some windows get one free pick and some get none. A quiet night costs you nothing; a forced pick does.

The market is our starting point

Prices already hold a lot of information. We start there and only make a call when our data adds real lift on top. When it doesn't, we pass or move to a safer line.

Graded in public

Every pick, wins and losses, settled by the official result at the price we posted. Backtests are labelled and never mixed into the live record.

Built like a trading system

Round Robin Sports Algo is proprietary. It was built by a data professional with 20 years in the fintech data industry, and spent three years in development, tested, backtested and retrained through many iterations, before the models went live. Picks are built from a consensus of models with calibrations that adjust as the season moves. It is not a chatbot answering questions.
