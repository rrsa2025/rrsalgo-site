Quick Hits track record

Every call is graded against the official box score the next morning, wins and losses. Regular season and postseason are kept apart, and so are live calls and backtests.

  • Live: posted before first pitch and graded the next morning against the official box score.
  • Backtest: simulated results, today's model calibration replayed on past games using only pre-game data.
  • Long shot: a single bet (never a parlay) at plus money. A $10 bet at +300 pays $30 profit if it hits.
  • Units: profit if you bet 1 unit (your normal bet size) on every call at the posted price.
  • Records read "X of N": calls won out of calls graded.
